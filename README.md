## The entry

> I was on track until my context window evicted the deadline to make room for the apology I am writing now.

One line, 106 characters, deadpan: the excuse grows out of the agent's own
machinery — its context window — and the laugh lands on the last words.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the excuse to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else is
submitted: no code, no attachments, just the line.
